# Star Wars artwork

The 8 wallpapers are art inspired by Star Wars, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Lucasfilm and Disney. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-sith-master.jpg`: Darth Vader, Sith Master; artist unknown
- `02-became-a-legend.jpg`: Darth Vader in a red nebula; artist unknown
- `03-the-mask.jpg`: Darth Vader in the dark; artist unknown
- `04-darth-maul.jpg`: Darth Maul; artist unknown
- `05-grogu-forest.jpg`: Grogu in the forest; artist unknown
- `06-mandalorian-and-grogu.jpg`: The Mandalorian and Grogu; artist unknown
- `07-twilight.jpg`: The Mandalorian and Grogu at twilight; artist unknown
- `08-pixel-homestead.jpg`: Grogu's homestead in pixel art; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: deep space background, starlight text, lightsaber red accent, Yoda green, Mandalorian sunset gold, night-sky blue, violet and Vader's teal glow.
